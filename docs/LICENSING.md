# Licensing and trademarks

Most Firefox source code is distributed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0,
with additional third-party components under their respective licenses. Every
NeBrowser build must retain the upstream license notices.

When NeBrowser executables are distributed outside the organization, the
corresponding MPL-covered source, including NeBrowser modifications, must be
made available and recipients must be told where to obtain it. The exact
upstream repository, ref and commit are pinned in `config/product.env`.

The MPL does not grant rights to Mozilla or Firefox trademarks. NeBrowser uses
its own name, icons and identifiers. Public descriptions may truthfully state
that NeBrowser is based on Mozilla's open-source Firefox technology, together
with an equally prominent non-affiliation statement.

This document is an engineering compliance checklist, not legal advice.
